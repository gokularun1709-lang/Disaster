Why SafeWorld

Built for the five minutes that matter most

Most disaster information lives in scattered PDFs, WhatsApp forwards and government portals that fall over exactly when traffic spikes. SafeWorld keeps the essentials — alerts, contacts, shelters, and what-to-do steps — in a lightweight app that works even on a weak connection.

Citizens can report incidents in seconds. Volunteers and local authorities can publish verified alerts and track relief resources from the same dashboard.
